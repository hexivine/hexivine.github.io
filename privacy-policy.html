Privacy Policy — BriefArc Uploader

Last updated: November 2025

BriefArc Uploader is a personal, single-user tool that uploads videos created by the channel owner to the owner's own YouTube channel via the official YouTube Data API.

  • It authenticates only as the channel owner, using a Google-issued OAuth token.
  • It collects, stores and shares no personal data with any third party.
  • The only data stored locally is the OAuth token used to authenticate the upload.
  • Contact: through the BriefArc YouTube channel.
